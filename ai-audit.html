Find the admin your team shouldn't be doing by hand

One hour with me, then a written report naming three automations for your business, each with a time estimate and a fixed quote. Built around the tools you already use, like Xero, ServiceM8, AroFlo, your CRM, email and calendar.

Book an audit

In a lot of small offices, good people lose hours each week copying details between systems, chasing paperwork and rebuilding the same reports. I'm Andrew MacPherson, and I set up simple automations around the tools you already use.

The AI time audit is a fixed price way to find out where that time goes and what it would cost to get it back.

What you get

  • A 60 minute session with me, online or at your office, where we walk through how work actually moves through your business.
  • A written report within five working days naming three automations that suit your business. For each one: what it does, which of your tools it connects, an estimate of the time it could save, and a fixed quote to build it.
  • The full fee back as credit if you go ahead with any build within 60 days.

To give you a feel for the estimates: if someone spends 20 minutes a day copying invoice details from email into Xero, automating that could free up close to two hours a week. That's only an example. The estimates in your report are worked out from your own numbers.

Who it's for

  • Accounting and bookkeeping practices where work around Xero still lives in email and spreadsheets
  • Property managers juggling maintenance requests, inspections and tenant follow ups
  • Trades offices running jobs through ServiceM8 or AroFlo, where quotes, job notes and invoices get retyped
  • Financial advisers losing hours to client onboarding and annual review prep
  • Any small business where someone says "I spend half my Monday on this"

If you need a custom app built from scratch, this isn't the right starting point. Send me an email instead.

Questions

What if you don't find three things worth automating?

Then the report says so, and lists what I did find with honest estimates. I'd rather tell you a job isn't worth automating than sell you something that won't pay for itself.

Do I have to buy a build?

No. The report is yours to keep. You can build it yourself, hand it to someone else or file it away. The credit is there if you want me to build it within 60 days.

What do you need from me beforehand?

A list of the software you use and a rough idea of the jobs that annoy you most. If someone else in the office does most of the admin, bring them along; they usually know where the time goes.

Is my data safe? Do you need logins?

I don't need logins or access to your systems for the audit. You share your screen or show me on your computer, and you decide what I see. If you go ahead with a build, we agree on access then, and I only ask for what the job needs.

Online or in person?

Either, same price. Online works anywhere in Australia. In person, I can come to you around Brisbane west, Ipswich and Springfield.

Bespoke AI for business

AI agents that take the repetitive work off your plate

MacPherson Ventures designs and builds practical AI automation for small and medium businesses in Australia and Vietnam. We start with where your time actually goes, then build agents that handle that work inside the tools you already use.

Let's talk

What we build

Each agent is set up for your business, your customers and your way of working. These are the jobs we're asked about most.

Google review replies

Agents that watch for new Google reviews across all your locations and draft replies in your voice, ready to approve or post automatically.

Reviews answered in minutes rather than days, with a consistent tone and very little staff time.

Voice AI agents

Inbound call handling in English or Vietnamese. The agent answers common questions, qualifies the caller and passes the details to your team, escalating when it should.

Fewer missed calls, and every lead reaches your team with the context they need.

Messaging automation

WhatsApp and Zalo assistants that handle enquiries, bookings and follow-ups, and hand over to a person when a conversation needs one.

Customers get an answer straight away, on the apps they already use, at any hour.

Workflow automation

Custom agents for the manual jobs that fill up the week: data entry, reporting, lead tracking and follow-ups.

Your team spends less time on admin and more on the work that needs a person.

About

MacPherson Ventures was founded by Andrew MacPherson, an Australian technology consultant with deep experience in enterprise systems and business analysis across Australian universities and financial services.

Andrew saw that plenty of businesses were ready to use AI but needed someone who understood both what the technology can do and how the business actually runs. Having lived and worked in Da Nang, he now works from Brisbane with businesses in Australia and Vietnam.

The aim is simple: AI agents that are practical, affordable and built around real problems rather than hype.
